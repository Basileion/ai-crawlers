OAI-SearchBot: what it is and how to block it

Operator: OpenAI · Purpose: AI / web search · Follows robots.txt: yes

Surfaces websites in ChatGPT search results. Sites opted out are not shown in ChatGPT search answers, though they can still appear as navigational links. OpenAI says robots.txt changes take about 24 hours to apply.

Source: https://platform.openai.com/docs/bots
