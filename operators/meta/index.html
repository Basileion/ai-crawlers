Meta crawlers

  • meta-externalagent: Crawls for use cases such as training foundation AI models or improving products by indexing content directly.
  • meta-webindexer: Improves Meta AI search results; allowing it helps Meta cite and link content in Meta AI responses.
  • meta-externalfetcher: Fetches individual links at a user's request, including agentic AI tasks. Meta says it may bypass robots.txt.
  • facebookexternalhit: Builds link previews (title, description, image) when links are shared on Facebook, Instagram or Messenger. May bypass robots.txt for security or integrity checks.

Source: https://developers.facebook.com/docs/sharing/webmasters/web-crawlers/, checked 2026-11-20.

AI Crawler Rules checks robots.txt against 20 AI and search crawler tokens, using each operator's own documentation (checked 2026-11-20). Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. robots.txt is a request, not access control; some user-initiated fetchers say they may not follow it.
