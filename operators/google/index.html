Google crawlers

  • Google-Extended: Standalone control token, no separate crawler. Manages whether content Google crawls may be used to train future Gemini models and for grounding in Gemini Apps and Vertex AI. Does not affect Google Search inclusion or ranking.
  • Googlebot: Google Search crawler (including Discover, Images, Video mode, News). Blocking it removes pages from Google Search crawling.
  • GoogleOther: Generic Google crawler used by product teams, for example one-off crawls for internal research and development. Does not affect a specific product.
  • Google-CloudVertexBot: Crawls requested by site owners for building Vertex AI Agents. No effect on Google Search. Also matches Googlebot rules.

Source: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/google-common-crawlers, checked 2026-11-20.

AI Crawler Rules checks robots.txt against 20 AI and search crawler tokens, using each operator's own documentation (checked 2026-11-20). Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. robots.txt is a request, not access control; some user-initiated fetchers say they may not follow it.
