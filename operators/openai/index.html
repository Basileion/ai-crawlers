OpenAI crawlers

  • GPTBot: Crawls content that may be used to train OpenAI's generative AI foundation models. Disallowing it signals content should not be used for training.
  • OAI-SearchBot: Surfaces websites in ChatGPT search results. Sites opted out are not shown in ChatGPT search answers, though they can still appear as navigational links. OpenAI says robots.txt changes take about 24 hours to apply.
  • ChatGPT-User: Visits pages for user actions in ChatGPT and Custom GPTs. Not used for automatic crawling. OpenAI says that because these actions are user-initiated, robots.txt rules may not apply.
  • OAI-AdsBot: Validates the safety of landing pages submitted as ads on ChatGPT. Only visits pages submitted as ads; data is not used to train foundation models.

Source: https://platform.openai.com/docs/bots, checked 2026-11-20.

AI Crawler Rules checks robots.txt against 20 AI and search crawler tokens, using each operator's own documentation (checked 2026-11-20). Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. robots.txt is a request, not access control; some user-initiated fetchers say they may not follow it.
