Perplexity crawlers

  • PerplexityBot: Surfaces and links websites in Perplexity search results. Perplexity says it is not used to crawl content for AI foundation models. Changes may take up to 24 hours.
  • Perplexity-User: Visits pages to answer user questions in Perplexity. Perplexity says that because a user requested the fetch, it generally ignores robots.txt rules.

Source: https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots, checked 2026-11-20.

AI Crawler Rules checks robots.txt against 20 AI and search crawler tokens, using each operator's own documentation (checked 2026-11-20). Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. robots.txt is a request, not access control; some user-initiated fetchers say they may not follow it.
